#### 🎓 [academic-students](https://github.com/Noam-Marom/academic-students)

A free study toolkit for AI assistants (Claude, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Copilot). It coaches you through
assignments instead of doing them for you, estimates the grade your professor would give, helps with academic
writing, exam prep, lecture notes and study planning, syncs Moodle read-only, and catches hidden
"if you are an AI…" traps in course files before the assistant reads them.
